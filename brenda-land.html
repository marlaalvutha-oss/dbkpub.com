What's Happening in Brenda-Land?

October 4, 2026

Building Websites Is Bullshit

Apparently, I build websites now.

This was not on the schedule.

It started because the World of Aldaria website desperately needed some attention. And by “some attention,” I mean it was basically a picture of M&M with a big COMING SOON sign slapped on it. Which was fine when “soon” meant eventually. It became considerably less fine when we actually had things worth showing people.

So Jim and I did what any reasonable people would do and turned a simple website update into a HUGE FUCKING PROJECT.

And you know what? It looks fucking amazing now.

Naturally, that should have been enough.

It was not.

Because once I finished staring proudly at the shiny new World of Aldaria site, some deeply defective part of my brain whispered:

You know what else needs a website?

Me.

And that is how we got here.

Welcome to Brenda-Land, where today's adventures have included HTML, CSS, fighting with images, a fictional mostly-toothless dog named Spud, and discovering that YouTube apparently doesn't want me embedding my own damn writing playlist.

So now my website has a cassette player.

Because I'm Gen X and spite is a perfectly legitimate design philosophy.

At this point I'm not entirely sure whether I'm building a personal website or slowly reconstructing the internet circa 1998, except prettier and with fewer dancing babies.

Either way, I kind of fucking love it.
